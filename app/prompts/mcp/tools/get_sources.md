The sources the user's new jobs come from — company career pages (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever) and RSS feeds (DOU, Djinni) — each with its last run (when, found / imported / skipped by their profile filters / duplicate / error). The server refreshes them on a schedule; jobs from them arrive with full text. Also filtered_out: saved jobs their current profile filters would no longer take (count and job_ids; hide them with dismiss_jobs only after they say yes). They edit the lists themselves at settings_url.
